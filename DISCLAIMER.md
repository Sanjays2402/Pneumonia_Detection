# Medical Disclaimer

This project is an **educational demonstration** of convolutional neural networks
applied to chest X-ray classification. It is **not** a medical device and has **not**
been reviewed or approved by any regulatory body (e.g. FDA, CE, CDSCO).

- Do **not** use this software to diagnose, treat, or make any clinical decision.
- Predictions may be incorrect, biased by the training data, or fail on images that
  differ from the Kaggle Chest X-Ray (Pneumonia) dataset it was trained on.
- The model outputs a probability, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified clinician,
  using appropriate clinical workflows, can diagnose pneumonia.

If you or someone else may have pneumonia, seek care from a licensed medical
professional.
