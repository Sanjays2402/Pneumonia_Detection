Detecting Pneumonia at an Early Stage

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs and may be caused by a virus, bacteria, or fungi. Early detection helps a patient seek timely care. This tool uses a convolutional neural network (CNN) to classify a chest X-ray as Normal or Pneumonia.
